# FLORN

**Discovery. Evidence. Knowledge.**

FLORN is an autonomous scientific intelligence designed to discover, verify and accumulate knowledge from data.

It investigates datasets, proposes candidate structures and relations, tests what the available evidence actually supports, and separates what is established from what remains hypothetical or unresolved.

FLORN does not require scientific authority to come from the AI system that generated an idea.

> **Models may propose. Agents may investigate. Memory may compose. None of them may certify.**

Scientific claims earn their status through an independent verification and certification layer.

---

## What FLORN does

FLORN can autonomously investigate structured scientific data and search for:

- exact relations and invariants;
- multivariate structures;
- conditional relations;
- ordered and temporal structures;
- bounded relationships;
- contrasts between environments or experimental groups;
- contradictions and counterexamples;
- causal relations only when the available evidence genuinely supports causal identification.

When the evidence is insufficient, FLORN can explicitly abstain rather than force a conclusion.

Its scientific outputs preserve:

- the proposition itself;
- epistemic status;
- verification status;
- scope;
- conditions;
- support;
- exceptions and counterexamples;
- provenance;
- proof references.

---

## Discovery without blind trust

Frontier AI models are becoming increasingly capable of generating hypotheses, writing code and conducting autonomous investigations.

Capability, however, is not the same thing as scientific authority.

FLORN separates them.

A generative model may help propose an idea.

An autonomous agent may decide what to investigate.

Previous knowledge may help construct new hypotheses.

But a claim does not become established simply because an intelligent system produced it.

FLORN independently verifies what can actually be supported by the available evidence.

---

## A real example

In a blind analysis of a dataset containing 14,194 observations, FLORN recovered and certified the following exact 12-variable identity:

`20·v01 + 11·v02 + 408·v03 + 28·v04 − 28·v05 + 14·v06 − 20·v08 − 14·v09 − 11·v10 − 408·v11 + 45·v13 − 45·v14 = 0`

**CERTIFIED · NON_CAUSAL**

- 14,194 rows checked
- 14,194 eligible rows
- 0 missing rows
- 0 violations

FLORN certified the exact relation while deliberately making no causal claim that the evidence did not justify.

This distinction is fundamental to the system.

## FLORN Insight

FLORN also includes a scientific interpretation layer.

While the deterministic FLORN view answers:

> **What has been established?**

**FLORN Insight** is designed to answer:

> **What should we understand from the verified results?**

It can connect related findings, identify recurring structures, highlight tensions and exceptions, propose hypotheses and formulate discriminating questions for further investigation.

Those interpretations do not acquire scientific authority merely because a frontier model produced them.

Established results remain grounded in FLORN's verified scientific objects.

---

## Cumulative knowledge

FLORN is designed so that scientific knowledge can persist beyond a single analysis.

Verified knowledge can retain its:

- expression;
- variables;
- scope;
- context;
- provenance;
- support;
- conditions;
- exceptions;
- epistemic status.

That knowledge can then become material for future investigations.

Memory may help FLORN search, compose and reason.

**Memory does not make a proposition true.**

New claims remain subject to verification.

---

## What-If and autonomous investigation

FLORN is more than a static analysis engine.

Its autonomous investigation layer can:

- choose promising lines of inquiry;
- examine competing structures;
- ask follow-up questions;
- use previous findings;
- construct new candidate hypotheses;
- explore counterfactual or What-If questions;
- return new candidates to verification.

This creates a loop between discovery, verification, knowledge and further investigation.

## CCAI

FLORN is a reference implementation of the proposed architecture:

**CCAI — Cumulative Certified Autonomous Intelligence.**

CCAI separates:

- generation;
- autonomous choice;
- memory;
- verification;
- epistemic authority.

Its central principle is simple:

> **The LLM may propose.  
> The Agent may choose.  
> Memory may compose.  
> None of them may certify.**

Learn more at **ccai.foundation**.

---

## Why this matters

As AI systems become capable of increasingly autonomous scientific and technical work, the limiting problem is no longer only:

> Can AI discover something?

It is increasingly:

> What can we trust, reproduce, inspect and reuse?

FLORN is being built around that problem.

The goal is not to make generative intelligence infallible.

The goal is to build an architecture in which intelligence can remain creative and powerful while scientific authority is earned through evidence.
